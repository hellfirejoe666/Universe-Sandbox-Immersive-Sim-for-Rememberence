## 💡 Design Notes

### LLM Prompt Structure
The `/oracle/llm` endpoint uses a two-part prompt:
1. **System prompt:** Establishes KaiMi persona (mystical archive AI)
2. **User prompt:** Includes roll context, question, astrological data

This ensures consistent voice while letting mechanics guide tone.

### EGO Bias Impact
EGO ranges from approximately -5 to +7 depending on sign combinations:
- High EGO (Dragon/Scorpio, Tiger/Aries): Strong self-confidence bias
- Low EGO (Rabbit/Cancer, Pig/Pisces): More humble/cautious rolls

This creates meaningful character differentiation in gameplay.
